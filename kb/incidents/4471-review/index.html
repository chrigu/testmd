Why the merge hid this

Decision 0001 accepted subject-only deduplication. The triage page still tells the desk to merge matching subjects and to merge when uncertain. Its interactive example already compares tenant/form causes instead. The words and executable demonstration disagree.

Subject similarity can help locate candidates, but it cannot establish a shared cause. Read the body, resolve the tenant’s locale and form, and compare the actual failure path. Matching subjects can conceal different failures; different subjects can describe the same failure. Missing tenant information or conflicting symptoms need investigation, not a forced merge into the largest ticket.

Do not rewrite accepted decision 0001. A replacement decision should record why subject-only merging failed, the evidence needed before merging, and how unresolved cases stay visible.
